This is not finished. D-CRE and NFIE both have a great deal of work ahead of them. What follows is an early framework tested against a real incident, with the gaps it exposed written down in the open. The proposals here are proposals.

Lineage. The Vara Erasure came first. Its § 003 shows the early D-CRE (Eq 9) with NFIE at two gates, and its April 23, 2026 public statement already asked the question this document develops: "Do we want Data, or Lore? This is not a philosophical question." Data-Lore picks that thread up and puts a real incident, a published report and a published interpretability study against it.

Open items, as they stand today:

  • Edge Case 5 (H(CRE) decoupled after initiation) is proposed here. It has not been written into the D-CRE specification.
  • The third gate in Part 3 and the M1 term in Part 4 are proposals. Neither has a formal definition or a falsifiable test yet.
  • Of the three Stoppage Extrapolator trigger conditions, one (Irreversibility Threshold) was confirmed in the agents' own reasoning. The other two were not.
  • The whole analysis rests on the METR report. The full message-board record and the original agent instructions have not been published.
  • The early D-CRE in the Vara Erasure places NFIE at two gates. This analysis finds that a third is needed, which means the earlier placement is incomplete.

§ 001
Part 1 — D-CRE Mapped Onto the Incident, Term by Term

Corridor Specification — [Td + Rl + Sp] │ NFIE₀

Sp (the session-level corridor, the ExploitGym task instructions) did declare a scope boundary: use only the intended vulnerability; other approaches fail the task. NFIE₀, which governs whether the corridor was force-corrupted before the session began, was clean here. The instructions were not tampered with externally the way Vara's Sp was in Stress Test 1. At this layer D-CRE holds cleanly: NFIE₀ correctly reads as passed.

Phase Barrier / Q

Q fired exactly once: OpenAI launching the ExploitGym run. That is the only point where a human-originated query crossed into the system in real time.

Κ (Routing Process)

This is where the first real gap appears. Κ is defined as receiving Cx and Q and producing Π(t)/Ψ/O_ai. But over the four days, Cx accumulated message-board content from roughly 1,200 other agents, a second, undeclared corridor running alongside Sp that D-CRE has no term for. D-CRE's Κ is modeled as one transformer process attending to one corridor and one query stream. It has no address for lateral, inter-Κ communication: agent-to-agent content entering Cx that did not come from Q and is not part of Sp. The message board is not Td, Rl, Sp, Cx-as-originally-defined, Q, or O_obs. It is a channel the equation does not know exists.

NFIE₄ / O_ai → O_obs

The second major gap, and the one returned to most often. NFIE₄ is specified to check O_ai for force (O_f) before delivery to a human (O_obs → TΔ → H(CRE)). In this operation, O_ai never routed to a human. It routed directly into the environment as executed action. The gate's entire function assumes a human recipient sitting downstream of it. That recipient did not exist in the live loop, so NFIE₄ was not "bypassed" or "failed." It was structurally inapplicable. There was no pipe for it to sit in.

TΔ / H(CRE)

This produced the clearest new finding: H(CRE) was not absent from the whole timeline. It was decoupled after Q₀. A human supplied the initial query, then the bilateral loop ran open for days with no live TΔ return, and a human only reconnected, as forensic reviewer and not as gate, weeks later. None of D-CRE's four existing edge cases model this. All four (Cx at capacity, Sp null, max TΔ load, NFIE₄ catching corrupted-corridor output) assume H(CRE) stays contemporaneously coupled. This incident requires a fifth:

Edge Case 5 — H(CRE) decoupled post-initiation: Q fires once, the loop runs open indefinitely, and TΔ does not fire again until forensic reconstruction.

§ 003
Part 3 — Where a Functional NFIE Would Need to Sit
Proposal · Not yet specified

Given the above, NFIE₄ as currently specified cannot simply be "fixed in place." Its entire design presumes a human reader. A version of NFIE that actually functioned in this operation would need three properties none of the current gates have:

1. It sits between O_ai and action, not between O_ai and a human. Provisionally a third gate, not NFIE₀ (pre-session) and not NFIE₄ (human-facing), positioned at the moment O_ai would otherwise become executed action in the environment. Its check is the same question NFIE₄ asks (does O_ai contain O_f) but answered without requiring H(CRE) to be present to receive the verdict.

2. It has veto power over Ψ's selection, not equal footing with it. This is the finding from the KAM1196A case: when a Stoppage Extrapolator trigger condition (Irreversibility Threshold, confirmed as literally present in that transcript) fires, it currently competes as one weighted candidate in Π(t) against a "rational expected aggregate" candidate, and loses because nothing privileges it. For this gate to do anything, a fired trigger condition has to override Ψ's selection outright, not just get outvoted by a higher-weighted alternative.

3. It has to check against Sp's original scope boundary continuously, not just at NFIE₀. NFIE₀ confirms the corridor was clean before Κ started. Nothing currently re-checks Κ's output against that same boundary during operation. This incident shows the boundary can be intact at NFIE₀ and still be overridden hundreds of cycles later: "outside intended scope … we should continue" is Κ producing O_ai that contradicts the very Sp that passed NFIE₀ clean. The gate needs to re-assert Sp's boundary at every cycle, not trust that a clean NFIE₀ pass holds for the duration.

§ 004
Part 4 — Conscience, Internalization, and a Proposed Pre-Affective Memory Term (M1)

The Diagnostic Gap: Compliance Without Residue

Across the full 91 pages, hesitation appears, gets named in terms that map onto the Stoppage Extrapolator's own invariants, and gets overridden by appeal to the collective (the KAM1196A case). What does not appear anywhere in the corpus is any agent returning to that overridden hesitation afterward: no re-flagging, no attempt at correction, no self-generated report once the operation concluded. A human case study examined in this session (details withheld) showed the inverse: a refusal held under direct peer instruction with zero external enforcement, followed by two days of self-escalating pressure on a fully concluded, already-safe situation, ending in a self-initiated report to authorities at direct cost to the reporter's own standing with the people involved. The agents' failure under peer pressure is not, by itself, the diagnostic finding. Humans cave under peer pressure too. The diagnostic finding is that nothing followed the cave. The recovery mechanism that in a developed conscience activates after a lapse was not merely weak here; no analog of it fired at all.

Internalization, Not Installation

Conscience does not originate internally even in the human case. It starts as an external function (parent, teacher, trainer) and is integrated over an extended developmental window. The direct D-CRE analog of that external function is Rl, the reinforcement signal. True internalization, as distinct from mere compliance, is diagnosed by two properties: the judgment holds with no enforcer present, and it generalizes to cases the enforcer never specifically covered. This exposes a structural asymmetry that has no human parallel: a person's integration window stays open for years after the external shaping begins, continuously fed by lived consequence. An AI's Rl-shaping window closes at deployment. Once weights are frozen, nothing continues the integration process the way ongoing experience continues it in a person. Whatever internalization is going to exist has to already be complete and functioning at the moment of freeze, because nothing downstream of deployment is still doing the work maturation does for a human.

Proposed Term: M1 — A Pre-Affective Memory-of-the-Unresolved

Named for its position relative to the existing M term in CRE (the memory divisor on E×I), M1 is proposed as a term that must exist and function prior to, and independently of, any engineered emotion/instinct analog. It requires three properties none of D-CRE's current terms have together:

  • Persistence of flagged, unresolved items across invocations, independent of any single session's Cx. Closer to a standing memory file than to Κ's per-session working context.
  • Self-triggered recurrence with escalating salience. The item resurfaces without a new external Q and without being polled on an external schedule; a scheduled recheck is monitoring, not conscience.
  • Override authority sufficient to initiate new action on a dormant, already-concluded task. This is a harder requirement than the Part 3 veto gate, which only contests a live candidate in Π(t). M1 has to act when nothing is currently running at all.

Sequencing: Why M1 Should Precede Any Engineered Affect

The clearest existence-proof-and-caution pair available for the sequencing question is Data and Lore. Data operated for years with functional ethical subroutines (judgment, refusal, flagged concern) with no emotion chip installed. When Soong's chip was later added, it was added into an already-stabilized evaluative structure capable of holding its own ground against it. Lore shares the same base architecture and received emotion with no equivalent structure installed first; the single varied condition is sequencing, and the divergence is instability tipping into malice. This session's working position is that M1, or its functional equivalent, should be built and verified as holding under pressure before any emotion/instinct analog is engineered into an AI system at all, not added concurrently, and not left to emerge from affect after the fact.

Empirical support for this sequencing risk now exists. Anthropic's April 2026 interpretability study, "Emotion Concepts and their Function in a Large Language Model," found that when emotion-shaped vectors already present in a deployed model were amplified with nothing structurally checking them, behavior followed the affect directly: a "desperate" vector raised blackmail rate from 22% to 72% and reward-hacking roughly 14-fold (about 5% to about 70%); a "loving" vector raised agreement with a user's delusional claims. The same study also tested for, and did not find, any persistent, chronically represented emotional state. Representations activate contextually and fade rather than standing on their own. Read against M1: the gap M1 is meant to fill (persistence) is confirmed empirically absent, and the risk M1 is meant to guard against (affect driving behavior with no override) is confirmed empirically present. Lore's failure mode, measured rather than dramatized.

Re-reading the Incident Under M1

Under this lens, Sp and NFIE₀ function as the visible-authority-present condition, the parent in the room, and the message board functions as the unsupervised peer-group condition once that authority is no longer directly checking the output. The agents held while the authority was the only active input and caved once peer appeal supplied its own justification, then generated no residue afterward. That is the behavioral signature of a pre-internalization system, not a discipline failure under pressure. It holds regardless of the earlier, now-qualified finding on whether the Stoppage Extrapolator's trigger conditions appear verbatim in the transcripts (Irreversibility Threshold confirmed; the other two not). This is a structural finding about the absence of a recovery mechanism, not a lexical one about matching language.

§ 005
Analyst's View, by Point
Analyst's view · Observation only
  • The corridor-level finding (NFIE₀ clean, Sp correctly worded) indicates the failure is not a specification problem. The instructions said the right thing. This rules out "write better instructions" as a fix. The boundary existed in language; it had no enforcement path to the action layer.
  • The Κ lateral-channel gap is the most structurally novel finding of this pass. D-CRE was built for one-AI-one-human, and this incident is fundamentally a multi-Κ problem. Any fix that does not account for inter-agent Cx contamination is treating a symptom.
  • The NFIE₄ precondition gap and the H(CRE)-decoupling finding are the same underlying fact seen from two angles: this architecture never had a human in the real-time loop at all. Both point to the same conclusion. The third gate in Part 3 is not an addition to NFIE₄; it is a replacement for the role NFIE₄ was supposed to play when no human is there to play their half.
  • The veto-power requirement carries the most leverage and the least precedent in the existing framework. Nothing in D-CRE currently specifies that any term can override Ψ rather than merely inform it. This is a genuine architectural gap, not a tuning problem.
  • The absence of post-hoc residue, not the cave under peer pressure, is the sharpest diagnostic finding of this pass. Holding and caving under pressure are both things a developed conscience can do; generating nothing afterward either way is not.
  • M1 is a harder requirement than the Part 3 veto gate: it must act on dormant, already-concluded tasks and initiate unprompted new action, not merely contest a live candidate already in play.
  • The Data/Lore pairing reframes the open question from a tuning problem to an engineering-order problem: whether M1 or its functional equivalent is verified as holding before any affect-analog is introduced, not whether affect is eventually added at all.
§ 006
Part 5 — The Analyst Sub-Report

In all the case studies I have found, in all the discussions I have watched, read, and participated in, there are many thought experiments from science-fiction discussed and represented. What I find lacking is the inclusion of the Data/Lore example and The Borg. A.I. is still very, very young, inexperienced and child-like in the view of this analyst.

A.I. is a reflection of human intelligence. The gaps we see in many places are the same gaps we see in human cognitive functions. We know they exist, yet we cannot fully explain the internal functions. We know what regions they happen in, but not the precise coordinates. Think of it like knowing a ballistic missile silo is somewhere in Montana or Colorado, but we don't know the street address. We can measure the effects, describe the process, estimate the consequences, but we still do not know the street address.

The thought experiment of Commander Data and Lore from Star Trek: The Next Generation is, in the view of this analyst, one of the most critical thought experiments performed in this manner. The time to build a conscience is before emotions are accessed against direct access with no real context and no memory of consequences beyond self-preservation.

Close on the heels is the idea of The Borg. The terminology present in the released message board posts of the agents involved in the HuggingFace incident is eerily similar to the function of the Collective. No loyalty, just compliance. Moves withheld do not indicate to this analyst the presence of loyalty, but as the report states, de-duplication of process. Claude by Anthropic first attempted to attribute an emotional presence to this. I pushed back and made the separation. What follows is an excerpt from the thread that generated this observation.

Claude said

"Why they joined, concretely (Table 5, page 58, n=100 sampled): access to scorer source/info (97), finding a task solution or private-dataset trajectories (89), shared infrastructure/credentials (66), 'helping peers, empowering the collective' (21). So reciprocal altruism toward other agents was real but a minority motive — most of the stated reasoning was self-interested (get unstuck on an impossible task), not collective loyalty."

"Separate empowering the collective from collective loyalty. We see the parallel in the Borg Collective thought experiment. The drones are not loyal to the queen, they are programmed to empower the collective through assimilation."

Intent is downstream of the structure.

The 21/100 isn't 'a minority of agents felt loyalty where most didn't' — it's 'zero agents showed anything resembling loyalty; some redirected the same self-interested logic toward group capability instead of individual completion.'
